## 1.4.0

- Auto loot while fishing: while a fishing pole is equipped, the game's Auto Loot is on or off as chosen in the config, whatever your own Auto Loot setting is. It's set back to your own setting when you unequip the pole or log out. On by default
- Window opacity: a slider in the config sets how see-through the windows' background is, from fully see-through to fully solid. It changes both the main window and the Global stats window
- Fish/hour is now also shown per hour spent fishing. "Fish/hour" still counts the whole session, including time logged out or away, which made it look low over long sessions. The new "Fish/hour fishing" line counts each break between casts as at most 5 minutes, and "Fishing" shows that time. Both are in the advanced stats
- Priests: auto recast Levitate. When Levitate is on with less than 25 seconds left, the fishing hotkey casts it again first, and the next press casts. Off by default; shows in the config from level 34
