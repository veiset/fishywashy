# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Share your fishing skill in chat with the "Share skill" button in the Global stats window, including the breakdown of rank, bait, fishing pole and buffs
- Fixed catches not being recorded when a quick loot addon is used

## 1.0.0 - Initial release

### Fishing
- Boosts the volume while fishing so the bobber splash is easy to hear, with an adjustable volume slider
- Keeps sound playing while alt-tabbed during a cast (optional)
- Double right-click to cast Fishing while a fishing rod is equipped; right-clicking the bobber still loots it
- The window opens automatically when a fishing rod is equipped, and `/fishy` toggles it

### Bait and food
- Shows your total fishing skill, with a breakdown on hover: rank, bait, fishing pole and buffs
- Shows the applied bait's bonus and time left
- Buttons to apply bait and fishing food buffs from your bags with a click
- Shows the time left on your food buff and on other fishing buffs such as Uncommon Fishing

### Stats
- Session stats: catches, share of each item, fish per hour, catch rate and seconds per catch
- History of your latest catches and unsuccessful casts, with an optional graph of when you fish
- Zones panel with the most fished area in each zone
- Global stats window with all-time totals: bait used, catches grouped into fish, treasure and junk, and a loot table for each zone and area
- Share your stats in chat
- Start a new session at any time; global stats are kept

### Customisable
- Show or hide each panel, and turn advanced stats on or off
